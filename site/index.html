How a day runs

  1. 01 · 00:00

    A place drops

    The same place for every crew on earth. Shared scarcity is the reason the daily format works at all.

  2. 02 · ALL DAY

    Everyone guesses

    Privately, on their own time, inside your crew’s day. Six time zones between you is fine. Nobody has to be awake at once.

  3. 03 · 23:59

    The day closes

    Everything reveals together, the best three scores count, and the streak moves. Somebody gets told they were the Navigator.

Three rules, and the reasons

Best three count

Averaging punishes a crew for having a casual member, which is exactly the person you want to keep. One bad guess never sinks the day.

Two players keep it alive

Requiring everyone would mean one person catching a flight ends a streak the whole family built. Hard to keep, harder to lose.

Nobody comes last

Navigator, Scout, Anchor, Wildcard. Recognition is categorical, not a ranking, so there is no seat at the table reserved for whoever is worst.

Where this actually is

It is not built. This page exists to find out whether the crew part is worth building, and it is the honest first step rather than a soft launch.

There is no waitlist position, no referral mechanic, and no number on this page telling you how many people are ahead of you. If enough people can picture their crew, I build it. If they cannot, I will say so and this page comes down.
