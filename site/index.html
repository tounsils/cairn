Three rules, and why

Built so the streak
is hard to lose.

Best three count

Averaging punishes a crew for having a casual member, who is exactly the person you want to keep. One bad guess never sinks the day.

Two players keep it alive

Requiring everyone means one person catching a flight ends a run the whole family built. Hard to keep, harder to lose.

Nobody comes last

Navigator, Scout, Anchor, Wildcard. Recognition is categorical rather than a ranking, so there is no seat reserved for whoever is worst.

Where this actually is

It is not built. This page exists to find out whether the crew part is worth building, and that is the honest first step rather than a soft launch.

There is no waitlist position, no referral mechanic, and no number telling you how many people are ahead of you. If enough people can picture their crew, I build it. If they cannot, I will say so and this page comes down.
